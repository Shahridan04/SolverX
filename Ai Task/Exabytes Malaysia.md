**Project Title: AI SME Digital Growth Advisor Tagline :** "Helping SMEs Discover Their Next Digital Step" 

## **Real Business Problem** 

Over 95% of Malaysian businesses are SMEs. 

Many know they should digitalise or adopt AI, but struggle to answer questions like: 

- Where should I start? 

- Which investment gives me the biggest impact? 

- Am I ready for AI? 

- Which tools should I implement first? 

- How much will it cost? 

- What ROI can I expect? 

As a result, many SMEs: 

- purchase the wrong tools 

- waste money 

- implement technology in the wrong sequence 

- fail to realise business value 

## **Challenge** 

Design an AI-powered advisor that can analyse an SME in under **5 minutes** and generate a personalised Digital & AI Transformation Blueprint. 

The solution should be practical enough that Exabytes can deploy it as a lead-generation and advisory tool for real customers.
